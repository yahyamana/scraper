Free placement, nothing out of pocket

If your store sees regular foot traffic, we will put a machine in it. There is no purchase, no lease and no monthly fee — we carry the equipment cost and the cash, and you earn on every withdrawal.

  • Machine, install and signage at zero cost
  • We supply and load the cash — your register is never touched
  • Surcharge revenue share paid out monthly
  • EMV and ADA compliant hardware, kept current

Cardholders who stop for cash tend to spend part of it before they leave. That second purchase is the real return on the floor space.

  • Per-terminal reporting you can hand to your accountant
  • Processing and settlement through our partners
